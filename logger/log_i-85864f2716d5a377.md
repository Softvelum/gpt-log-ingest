## Short description

Diagnostic text forwarded at information level.

## Trigger condition

A caller forwards text from Linux video-acceleration library information callbacks in supported Quick Sync/VA-API builds with callback support; authorization diagnostics configured at this level; or RIST notice/information callbacks.

## Explanation

The text is printed as-is. RIST removes one final newline first. Authorization logging also stores its diagnostic when logging is enabled. These forwarding paths do not share a single failure consequence.

## Possible causes

- The originating library or authorization component emitted the supplied diagnostic.

## What to check

1. Identify the originating component from the text and nearby context; the format itself contains no component prefix.

## Recommended action

Follow the meaning of the supplied library, authorization, or exception diagnostic.
