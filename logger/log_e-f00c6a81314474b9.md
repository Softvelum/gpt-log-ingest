# Icecast session stopped for unavailable advertisement

Product: Nimble Streamer
Component: Advertising
Logging function: log_e
Log level: error
Documentation ID: log_e-f00c6a81314474b9

## Log pattern

```text
stop on advertising content error id=%s uri=%s
```

## Parameters

- Argument 1 — Content ID: Text identifying the advertising content.
- Argument 2 — Content URI: Text giving the original advertising content location.

## Log examples

Illustrative examples; these are not captured server logs.

```text
stop on advertising content error id=ad-01 uri=https://media.example/ad.mp4
```

## Short description

Icecast session stopped for unavailable advertisement.

## Trigger condition

The scheduled advertisement is not ready, cannot be waited for, and its error policy does not permit skipping.

## Explanation

Advertising sample selection requests termination of the playback session.

## Possible causes

- The scheduled advertisement is not ready, cannot be waited for, and its error policy does not permit skipping.

## What to check

1. Restore the advertised content or review the rule policy that stops playback on failure.

## Recommended action

Restore the advertised content or review the rule policy that stops playback on failure.
