# Video initialization structure cannot be loaded

Component: Fragmented media to transport stream packaging

Log level: error

## Log examples

Illustrative examples; these are not captured server logs.

## Short description

Video initialization structure cannot be loaded.

## Trigger condition

Parsing or accessing AVC or HEVC video initialization data raises an MP4-structure error.

## Explanation

Video transport-header construction fails. The shared text does not distinguish AVC from HEVC.

## Possible causes

- Parsing or accessing AVC or HEVC video initialization data raises an MP4-structure error.

## What to check

1. Check initialization-segment completeness and its MP4 video-track structure.

## Recommended action

Replace malformed or mismatched initialization data.
