## Short description

WHEP helper process launch failed.

## Trigger condition

Creating the automatically managed WHEP helper process fails during listener initialization.

## Explanation

The listener socket is closed and initialization fails. A configured helper port bypasses automatic process launch.

## Possible causes

- The WHEP helper executable cannot be started from the configured executable directory.

## What to check

1. Check the installed WHEP helper and adjacent process-creation diagnostics.

## Recommended action

Restore the matching helper executable and its ability to run, then restart WHEP service.
