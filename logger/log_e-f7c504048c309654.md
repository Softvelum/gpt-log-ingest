## Short description

Child process connection timed out.

## Trigger condition

The child is still running more than five seconds after launch, while its communication session remains in the connecting state.

## Explanation

The owning worker marks the attempt as failed and closes the session. A persistent worker resets for a later request; a replaceable worker exits. Stopping a running child waits briefly and can terminate it.

## Possible causes

- The child has not completed the local connection transition within five seconds.

## What to check

1. Check whether the child starts and connects to its assigned local endpoint promptly.

## Recommended action

Investigate the child startup and local connection failure.
