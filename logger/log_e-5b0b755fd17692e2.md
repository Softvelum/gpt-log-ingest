## Short description

Annex B frame assembly buffer write failed.

## Trigger condition

Storing an initial frame part, appending a continuation, or starting the next timestamped frame fails in the combined buffer.

## Explanation

The assembly operation returns false and normal input frame handling resets video state. The combined buffer has a 10 MiB maximum. In the third occurrence the previous frame may already have been processed before storage of the next one fails.

## Possible causes

- The combined frame data could not be written within available buffer capacity.

## What to check

1. Check accumulated frame size, timestamp boundaries, and memory diagnostics.

## Recommended action

Correct unexpectedly large or unbounded frame assembly or resolve allocation failure.
