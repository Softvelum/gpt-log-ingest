## Short description

CUDA device enumeration failed.

## Trigger condition

Retrieving the CUDA device list fails.

## Explanation

NVIDIA context-manager initialization returns failure.

## Possible causes

- The device-count query fails, or retrieving one of the enumerated devices fails.

## What to check

1. Check driver availability and any preceding device-index failure.

## Recommended action

Restore CUDA enumeration and retry startup.
