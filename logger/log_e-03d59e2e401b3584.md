## Short description

WHIP control output monitoring could not stop.

## Trigger condition

After pending control data has drained and new requests have been processed, changing the control socket back to input/close monitoring fails.

## Explanation

The control socket is closed, pending starts are marked finished, pending data is cleared, and active WHIP publications are removed and their stream registrations released.

## Possible causes

- The event monitor rejects the control-socket update.

## What to check

1. Check for a concurrent control-connection failure and nearby event-monitor errors.

## Recommended action

Restore the helper connection; investigate repeated monitoring failures.
