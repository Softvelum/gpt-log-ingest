# Child process forcibly terminated on Windows

## Short description

Child process forcibly terminated on Windows.

## Trigger condition

On Windows, the child has not stopped within the caller-supplied timeout.

## Explanation

A termination request is issued and the helper then waits for the child.

## Possible causes

- The child exceeded its permitted shutdown waiting period.

## What to check

1. Check why the child did not stop within its caller-defined timeout.

## Recommended action

Investigate delayed child shutdown if forced termination is unexpected.
