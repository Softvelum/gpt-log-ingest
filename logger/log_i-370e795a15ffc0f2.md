## Short description

DVR archive batch reload was interrupted.

## Trigger condition

A loader notices its coordinating worker has an exit request after an archive load.

## Explanation

That loader stops taking further archives.

## Possible causes

- A loader notices its coordinating worker has an exit request after an archive load.

## What to check

1. Check shutdown activity if the reload was expected to continue.

## Recommended action

Check shutdown activity if the reload was expected to continue.
