## Short description

Advertising worker creation failed.

## Trigger condition

Starting the advertising worker fails during initial license verification or a later license refresh.

## Explanation

The license worker enters its exit state and returns; advertising processing is not started.

## Possible causes

- Starting the advertising worker fails during initial license verification or a later license refresh.

## What to check

1. Check the earlier thread-creation error and available process resources.

## Recommended action

Check the earlier thread-creation error and available process resources.
