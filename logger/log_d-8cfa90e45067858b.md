## Short description

Media worker thread stopped.

## Trigger condition

A media sender or camera worker has detected an exit request, left its processing loop, and reached the end of its thread routine.

## Explanation

The worker is about to return and will perform no further event-loop processing. General client sender workers have deleted their active sender objects before this message. SRT listen and pull/rendezvous input workers have applied an empty camera configuration to remove their managed inputs; pull workers also remove primary-connection probes. The MPEG-TS, constant-bitrate, SRT server, and Zixi variants log this immediately after leaving their loops. SRT variants require SRT support, and the UDP and SRT constant-bitrate variants are available on non-Windows builds. This is a worker lifecycle message, and does not by itself identify why shutdown was requested.

## Possible causes

- A media sender or camera worker has detected an exit request, left its processing loop, and reached the end of its thread routine.

## What to check

1. Confirm that the worker exit was expected and correlate it with the preceding exit-request and shutdown messages. If service should continue, investigate the reason its worker was asked to exit.
