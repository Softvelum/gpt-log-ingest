## Short description

Advertising timers see backward wall-clock change.

## Trigger condition

The current wall-clock time is earlier than the last timer check.

## Explanation

All active advertising timers are removed and fired immediately with the current time, then the timer-check baseline is reset.

## Possible causes

- The current wall-clock time is earlier than the last timer check.

## What to check

1. Check for a backward system-clock adjustment and timer-related behavior.
