## Short description

Splice information does not fit one transport packet.

## Trigger condition

The SCTE-35 sample plus its one-byte pointer field exceeds the payload remaining after the transport header.

## Explanation

The function returns without advancing the output offset or copying the splice sample. The continuity counter has already advanced.

## Possible causes

- The splice section exceeds the single-packet payload capacity supported by this writer.

## What to check

1. Check the splice section size; this path supports at most 183 sample bytes in one 188-byte transport packet.

## Recommended action

Use a splice section that fits this path or a writer that supports section fragmentation.
