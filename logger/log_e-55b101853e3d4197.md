## Short description

CPIX signature validation failed.

## Trigger condition

Response signature validation returns failure under the configured validation mode.

## Explanation

The KeyOS response is rejected before key decryption.

## Possible causes

- Response signature validation returns failure under the configured validation mode.

## What to check

1. Check the CPIX digest and signature, the trusted signing certificate, and the validation mode.

## Recommended action

Check the CPIX digest and signature, the trusted signing certificate, and the validation mode.
