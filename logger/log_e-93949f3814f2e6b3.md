Illustrative examples; these are not captured server logs.

## Short description

Whisper audio frame queue full.

## Trigger condition

The resampled audio queue already contains at least 2048 frames when another frame arrives.

## Explanation

The oldest queued frame is discarded, then the new frame is queued. Lost audio can create a gap in transcription.

## Possible causes

- The resampled audio queue already contains at least 2048 frames when another frame arrives.

## What to check

1. Check whether transcription is consuming audio as quickly as frames arrive.
