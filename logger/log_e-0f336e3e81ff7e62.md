## Short description

Cannot append audio to Icecast output chunk.

## Trigger condition

Writing a completed AAC or MP3 frame into the Icecast output chunk fails.

## Explanation

The ingest session is shut down.

## Possible causes

- Writing a completed AAC or MP3 frame into the Icecast output chunk fails.

## What to check

1. Check output chunk size and buffer allocation.
