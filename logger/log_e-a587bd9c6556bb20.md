## Short description

Advertising rules response omits content definitions.

## Trigger condition

A main rules response produces nonempty rules without containing a contents section.

## Explanation

The rules are still applied; content references may already have been replaced by empty content objects.

## Possible causes

- A main rules response produces nonempty rules without containing a contents section.

## What to check

1. Include the contents section defining the media referenced by these rules.

## Recommended action

Include the contents section defining the media referenced by these rules.
