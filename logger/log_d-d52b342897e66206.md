## Short description

WebSocket client requests connection closure.

## Trigger condition

A complete masked close frame has been received and its payload decoded.

## Explanation

The command parser returns failure to end the connection. The wording does not guarantee a nonempty close payload.

## Possible causes

- A complete masked close frame has been received and its payload decoded.

## What to check

1. Check whether the player intentionally closed its WebSocket connection.

## Recommended action

No action is required for an expected close.
