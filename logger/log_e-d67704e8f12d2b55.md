## Short description

HEVC output frame buffer write failed.

## Trigger condition

Converting a HEVC Annex B frame cannot reserve or append the length-prefixed VPS, SPS, PPS, or another NAL unit.

## Explanation

The converter returns false before delivering the completed frame. The destination buffer has a 10 MiB maximum. Direct frame processing and pending-frame flush reset video state after propagated failure; interleaved processing returns failure through its own caller.

## Possible causes

- The output NAL length or payload could not be stored.

## What to check

1. Check frame sizes, the conversion buffer limit, and preceding allocation diagnostics.

## Recommended action

Correct oversized or malformed input or resolve the allocation problem.
