## Short description

Scenario connection listener could not start.

## Trigger condition

Creating the scenario manager listening socket on the loopback address with an automatically assigned port fails.

## Explanation

The scenario manager thread returns before creating or handling scenarios.

## Possible causes

- Socket creation, binding, listening or event-monitor registration fails.

## What to check

1. Check earlier socket errors and local socket resources.

## Recommended action

Correct the reported socket failure and restart scenario management.
