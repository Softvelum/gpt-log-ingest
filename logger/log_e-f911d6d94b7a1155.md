## Short description

SLDP text response cannot be framed.

## Trigger condition

Framing a generated status or time response fails.

## Explanation

The response cannot be added to the output buffer and the operation fails. WebSocket framing can reject text above 65,535 bytes or fail a buffer write.

## Possible causes

- Framing a generated status or time response fails.

## What to check

1. Check response size, buffer-allocation errors, and any preceding WebSocket size message.

## Recommended action

Reduce excessive status content or resolve the output-buffer failure.
