## Short description

WHIP helper process launch failed.

## Trigger condition

Creating the automatically managed WHIP helper process fails while initializing the listener.

## Explanation

The listener socket is closed and WHIP helper initialization fails. An explicitly configured helper port bypasses this automatic launch.

## Possible causes

- The WHIP helper executable cannot be started from the configured executable directory.

## What to check

1. Check the installed WHIP helper executable and nearby process-creation diagnostics.

## Recommended action

Restore the matching helper executable and its ability to run, then restart WHIP service.
