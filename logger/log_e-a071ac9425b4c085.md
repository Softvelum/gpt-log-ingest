## Short description

RTMP aggregate has an incomplete submessage header.

## Trigger condition

Fewer than 15 bytes remain while reading another aggregate submessage, which cannot hold its tag header and back-pointer.

## Explanation

The aggregate is rejected and connection processing fails. Earlier submessages may already have been processed.

## Possible causes

- Fewer than 15 bytes remain while reading another aggregate submessage, which cannot hold its tag header and back-pointer.

## What to check

1. Check the aggregate message length and the last submessage framing.

## Recommended action

Correct the encoder aggregate framing or disable its malformed aggregation.
