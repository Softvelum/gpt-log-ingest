## Short description

Reconstructed video exceeds its transport buffer.

## Trigger condition

A transport writer reports insufficient output capacity while writing an AVC, HEVC, or MPEG-2 video sample.

## Explanation

The fragmented-media output chunk reconstruction returns failure. This shared message covers video-only and combined audio/video chunks.

## Possible causes

- A transport writer reports insufficient output capacity while writing an AVC, HEVC, or MPEG-2 video sample.

## What to check

1. Check sample size and transport overhead against the output-buffer capacity.

## Recommended action

Resolve the output sizing or allocation problem.
