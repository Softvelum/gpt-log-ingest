
- Argument 1 — Quality range: Driver-reported quality range, integer; no unit.

## Short description

VAAPI quality range reported.

## Trigger condition

The quality-range query succeeds and reports a range greater than one.

## Explanation

The available range is reported; the requested quality setting is read and applied separately.
