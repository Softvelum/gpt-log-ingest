## Short description

Response sender has no remaining output.

## Trigger condition

The prepared-response sender is called after headers and body are fully sent and no output chain remains.

## Explanation

The session is marked finished.

## Possible causes

- The prepared-response sender is called after headers and body are fully sent and no output chain remains.

## What to check

1. Correlate completion and writable-event messages; a completed response should not re-enter this output path.

## Recommended action

Correlate completion and writable-event messages; a completed response should not re-enter this output path.
