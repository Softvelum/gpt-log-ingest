# Icecast advertising audio sample failed

Product: Nimble Streamer
Component: Advertising
Logging function: log_e
Log level: error
Documentation ID: log_e-864a25a57e7c6a5f

## Log pattern

```text
failed to get advertising sample for content id=%s uri=%s
```

## Parameters

- Argument 1 — Content ID: Text identifying the advertising content.
- Argument 2 — Content URI: Text giving the original advertising content location.

## Log examples

Illustrative examples; these are not captured server logs.

```text
failed to get advertising sample for content id=ad-01 uri=https://media.example/ad.mp4
```

## Short description

Icecast advertising audio sample failed.

## Trigger condition

Reading the next audio sample from the advertisement fails.

## Explanation

The reader is released and playback is told to finish.

## Possible causes

- Reading the next audio sample from the advertisement fails.

## What to check

1. Check the advertising audio content for malformed or unsupported samples.

## Recommended action

Check the advertising audio content for malformed or unsupported samples.
